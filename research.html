Research

Research that has to survive contact with reality.

Our research program develops the architectures behind the ZebraFlux AI Character Engine. We run falsifiable experiments, keep what survives, and discard the rest.

Program

What we investigate.

Direction 01

Dual-system character architectures

How to couple a fast, continuously-evaluating system with a slower deliberative one so that a character's behavior stays coherent across an entire interaction — not just fluent for a single line.

Direction 02

State-driven behavior

Characters whose reactions are selected by persistent internal state — mood, history, consequence — rather than by the immediate prompt alone. The same input should be able to draw different reactions depending on what the character carries into the moment.

Direction 03

Efficient training

Methods for reaching capable small models on limited compute: training regimes, data curation, and evaluation designs that extract the most signal per GPU-hour.

Method

How we work.

Every claim we care about gets a control condition. We design ablations that can kill our own hypotheses — shuffled inputs, deranged signals, yoked baselines — and we report what fails alongside what survives. Null results redirect the program; they don't get buried.

We publish selectively and file intellectual property before disclosing novel methods. Collaboration inquiries from research groups and industry are welcome.
