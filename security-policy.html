Security

Security policy.

How to report a vulnerability to ZebraFlux, and what you can expect from us in return.

Responsible disclosure

ZebraFlux, Inc. welcomes reports from security researchers and the public. If you believe you have found a security vulnerability affecting zebraflux.com or a ZebraFlux service, please tell us first and give us a reasonable opportunity to correct it before disclosing it publicly.

How to report

Email msallam@zebraflux.com. For sensitive details, encrypt your report with our OpenPGP key:

  • Public key: zebraflux.com/.well-known/openpgp-key.asc
  • Fingerprint: 2780 4690 9DA7 C09E 35AA DDC6 DFA6 B9EA 1902 43B4

Please include a description of the issue, the affected URL or system, steps to reproduce, and the impact as you understand it.

What we ask

  • Make a good-faith effort to avoid privacy violations, data destruction, and service disruption.
  • Do not access, modify, or delete data that does not belong to you; stop and report as soon as you encounter it.
  • Do not run denial-of-service attacks or automated scanning that degrades our services.
  • Do not use social engineering, phishing, or physical attacks.

Our commitments

  • We will acknowledge your report within three business days.
  • We will keep you informed as we investigate and remediate.
  • We will not pursue legal action against researchers who act in good faith under this policy.
  • With your permission, we will credit your contribution on our acknowledgements page.

Our machine-readable disclosure file is published as security.txt (RFC 9116).
