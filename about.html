A veteran-owned AI research company.

ZebraFlux, Inc. is a Michigan corporation founded in 2024. We build AI systems and we engineer software — research and production under one roof.

The company

What ZebraFlux is.

Incorporated in Michigan in August 2024. Led by Chief Executive Officer Jonathan Nausley and Chief Technology Officer Maximus Sallam. Two sides that reinforce each other: a research program developing the ZebraFlux AI Character Engine, and a services practice delivering software engineering and cybersecurity solutions for government and business clients. Research keeps our engineering at the frontier; engineering keeps our research honest about what works in production.

We test claims, not demos.

Our research runs on falsifiable experiments: control conditions, ablations, preregistered comparisons. A result that cannot survive its own control does not ship.

We build for production conditions.

Our background is production infrastructure — systems that must operate under real constraints. That discipline carries into everything we deliver.

We are direct.

We say what a system does, what it does not do, and what the evidence shows. No inflated claims.
