About Us

A veteran-owned AI research company.

ZebraFlux, Inc. is a Michigan corporation founded in 2024. We build AI systems and we engineer software — research and production under one roof.

The company

What ZebraFlux is.

ZebraFlux, Inc. was incorporated in Michigan in August 2024. It is a veteran-owned company led by Chief Executive Officer Jonathan Nausley and Chief Technology Officer Maximus Sallam.

The company has two sides that reinforce each other. The research side develops the ZebraFlux AI Character Engine — real-time AI software for believable interactive characters in video games, virtual worlds, and companion applications. The services side provides broad software engineering and cybersecurity solutions for government and business clients.

That pairing is deliberate. Research keeps our engineering at the frontier; engineering keeps our research honest about what works in production.

How we work.

We test claims, not demos. Our research program is built around falsifiable experiments: control conditions, ablations, and preregistered comparisons. A result that cannot survive its own control does not ship.

We build for production conditions. Our engineering background is production infrastructure — systems that must operate under real constraints, not in notebooks. That discipline carries into everything we deliver.

We are direct. We say what a system does, what it does not do, and what the evidence shows. No inflated claims.
