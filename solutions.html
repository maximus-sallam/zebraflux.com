Product · In development

ZebraFlux AI Character Engine

Real-time AI software for believable interactive characters in video games, virtual worlds, and companion applications.

Behavior from state, not scripts.

Most interactive characters run on dialogue trees or stateless model calls. The Character Engine couples a fast, continuously-evaluating system with a slower deliberative one, so a character can react in the moment and still hold a coherent thread across an interaction.

  • Persistent internal state across a session
  • Reactions selected by history and consequence
  • Real-time operation for games and virtual worlds

Video games

Non-player characters with continuity across sessions — not one-line performances.

Virtual worlds

Inhabitants that remember, develop, and respond to a living environment.

Companion applications

Consistent, long-horizon interaction instead of reset-every-session chat.
