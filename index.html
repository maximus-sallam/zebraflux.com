Who we are

An AI research company with an operator's discipline.

ZebraFlux, Inc. is a Michigan corporation founded in 2024 and led by CEO Jonathan Nausley and CTO Maximus Sallam. We work where research meets production: novel AI architectures on one side, hardened systems engineering on the other.

Our research program targets interactive characters whose behavior emerges from internal state, consequence, and memory — not scripted dialogue trees. Our services practice applies the same rigor to custom software and cybersecurity engagements.

Research

We research what we ship.

Our internal research program investigates dual-system AI architectures: a fast, continuously-evaluating system coupled with a slower deliberative one. The goal is characters with genuine behavioral continuity — persistence of state across an interaction, not just fluent next sentences.

We run falsifiable experiments, publish selectively, and feed what survives into the Character Engine. Read about our research program →
